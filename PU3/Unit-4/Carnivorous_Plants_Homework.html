🎤 Speak!

Close your notes. Explain how ONE trap works in 3–4 sentences. Use need to or have to at least once.
Example answers (for checking your ideas)

Venus flytrap: A Venus flytrap has leaves like jaws and tiny teeth along the edges. It needs to catch insects to get extra nutrients. When an insect gets inside, the jaws snap shut. The plant breaks down its prey with digestive enzymes.

Pitcher plant: A pitcher plant looks like a tall, tube-shaped container. It has colourful patterns to attract insects. Insects fall into the tube and cannot escape the slippery walls. The plant has to get extra nutrients to survive.

For open-ended questions, other accurate explanations are also correct.
