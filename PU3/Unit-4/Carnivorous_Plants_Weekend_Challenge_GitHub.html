Mission 03 • Reading

📖 Read like a detective

Carnivorous plants grow in many places, including wet bogs, swamps and tropical rainforests. There are more than 600 different kinds of carnivorous plants. Many grow in poor soil that does not have enough nutrients.

To survive, they catch insects and sometimes small animals. Their prey gives them extra nutrients. Some plants use bright colours or sweet smells to attract insects. They use digestive enzymes to break down their food.

Mission 07 • Compare

⚡ Active or passive?

Active trap: the plant moves to catch prey. Passive trap: the trap does not need to move to catch prey.

Think about the movement described in the video.

Remember: a sundew's prey first gets stuck, and then the leaf may slowly wrap around it.
